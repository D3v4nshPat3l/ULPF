Integrity

            
Every event carries a fingerprint over its own canonical form plus a link to its predecessor, and the chain is anchored by Ed25519-signed checkpoints. Verification re-hashes the retained window and walks the links.

                append-only and is not touched, so the original bytes stay retrievable and
